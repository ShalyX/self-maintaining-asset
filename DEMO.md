# Demo — The Self-Maintaining Asset

The live prototype and all public transactions are real NEAR testnet operations. The completion document is a labelled fixture; no physical repair occurred.

## Story

A water point's repair money and records should survive its operator. The passport binds its dedicated account, reserve and service record to a transferable NFT.

Show the existing live demo at https://self-maintaining-asset.vercel.app . Start with the problem and promise, then inspect revenue allocation, the approved technician quote and completion digest, the PAID order, and ownership history. The payoff is the same keyless child account and remaining money under the new owner's authority.

## Recorded proof
